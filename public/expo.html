About Nigeria Health Expo

A platform for knowledge, networking and business.

Nigeria Health Expo is a premier healthcare exhibition and conference company that brings together healthcare professionals, medical practitioners, and industry experts from Nigeria and around the world.

Our vision

To become the leading healthcare exhibition and conference company in West Africa, promoting innovation, excellence, and partnerships in the healthcare industry.

Our mission

To provide a platform for healthcare professionals, medical practitioners, and industry experts to share knowledge, showcase innovations, and explore business opportunities in the healthcare sector.

Why choose us

Experience the ultimate healthcare event in Nigeria.

When and where is Nigeria Health Expo 2027?

26–28 April 2027 at Oriental Hotel, Victoria Island, Lagos.

Who should attend?

Healthcare professionals, medical practitioners and industry experts from Nigeria and around the world, including doctors, nurses, hospital administrators and procurement officers.
